Which one would you ship?

  • Tie on metrics → ship the simple one. Logistic regression wins on F1 and explains itself.
  • Non-linear interactions matter → boosting usually buys 1–2 points on tabular data.
  • Latency & memory critical → linear or a single tree; forests are 100× larger.
  • Regulated decisions → linear or shallow tree, so you can justify every rule.

What the numbers say here

The data is largely linearly separable along academic strength, so a hyperplane already captures most of the structure — that is why the simple model holds its own against ensembles. Trees still rank marginally better by AUC, which would matter if we scored and ranked students rather than thresholding them.

Deployment rule used here: best hold-out F1, tie broken in favour of interpretability.
