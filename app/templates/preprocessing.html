Preprocessing — one frozen pipeline

Cleaning, imputation, scaling and encoding live in a single fitted transformer (src/preprocessing.py → models/pipeline.pkl), reused verbatim at serve time.

The five steps

  1. Clean — drop StudentID, IsAnomaly and Salary Package (label leakage: salary only exists once placed).
  2. Impute — median for numerics ({{ n_num }} columns), most-frequent for categories.
  3. Scale — StandardScaler on continuous features.
  4. Encode — one-hot for {{ n_cat }} categorical columns, ordinal for CollegeTier (Tier3→Tier1) and CGPA_Tier (Low→Mid→High).
  5. Split & freeze — stratified 80/20, random_state=42; the fitted transformer is pickled.

Why this matters

Training–serving skew is the bug that breaks the most production ML systems: features prepared one way in training and another way at serve time.

Feature stores exist to keep one canonical definition of every feature. Here the fitted pipeline.pkl plays that role: fit once on training data, then transform() everywhere else.

Never fit the scaler on the whole dataset — that leaks test statistics into training. Never one-hot at train time and label-encode at serve time.

from sklearn.compose import ColumnTransformer
preprocessor = ColumnTransformer([
    ("num",  Pipeline([("impute", SimpleImputer(strategy="median")),
                        ("scale", StandardScaler())]),               numerics),
    ("tier", Pipeline([("impute", SimpleImputer(strategy="most_frequent")),
                        ("ordinal", OrdinalEncoder(categories=tiers))]), ["CollegeTier", "CGPA_Tier"]),
    ("cat",  Pipeline([("impute", SimpleImputer(strategy="most_frequent")),
                        ("onehot", OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown="ignore"))]), categoricals),
])
preprocessor.fit(X_train)          # fit on TRAIN only
joblib.dump(preprocessor, "models/pipeline.pkl")   # frozen artefact
